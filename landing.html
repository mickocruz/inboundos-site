FAQ

Questions we get
asked a lot.

"Do I need to film everything myself?"

Yes. Positioning, scripting, the agents, the review step, and the DM conversion sequence are ours. The camera is yours. Nobody has worked out how to outsource being the person on screen.

"How much time does this take per week?"

One 60-minute call a week and one filming session a month that you batch in advance. Everything else happens in messages.

"I'm not technical. Will I be able to use the AI agents?"

They are built for you, so there is nothing to configure. You type one line and a full script comes back in your voice. If you can send a DM you can run this.

"We already tried ChatGPT. Why would this be different?"

ChatGPT starts empty every time you open it. What we build is everything that has to exist around a model before it is useful: your company database, your voice data, the agents, the batching workflow, the review step, and the DM sequences. The model is the cheapest part of that list.

"Will the content actually sound like me?"

We train it on your calls, posts, frameworks, offers, objections, and the stories you already tell on sales calls. Then you review the hooks before anything goes out. Clients usually tell us it sounds like them after about two weeks of corrections.

"What if I already have an editor or VA?"

Good, keep them. They get content pillars, scripts, a batching rhythm, review standards, and a CTA flow to work from, which means they spend their time executing instead of guessing what you wanted.

"Do I keep everything after 90 days?"

All of it. The agents, the workflows, the prompt library, the content calendar, the review standards, and the DM sequences. That is what makes it an install rather than a subscription.

"Why not just hire a content agency?"

An agency charges $3K to $10K a month and takes the system with them when the relationship ends. We build inside your business and leave it there after 90 days. You pay for the build once.

"What if it's not working?"

Then we keep working until it does. We are watching what converts from the first week, so problems surface early enough to fix cheaply. You will not be left staring at a dashboard trying to work out what went wrong.

"How much does this cost?"

The 90-day build is a one-time cost. What it comes to depends on how much data we are ingesting, how many agents you need, and how messy the integrations are. We price it on the discovery call. There is no monthly fee attached to it.
