FAQ

You've got questions.
We've got answers.

"Does this replace the tools we already use?"

Some tools we connect to permanently, some you eventually retire. The audit decides which. Anything regulated, your accounting software especially, we only read from. We never write to it. Your existing tools stay live and untouched during the build, and you only retire something once you've confirmed everything carried over.

"What happens to our existing data?"

Migration is part of week one. We export from your current systems, map the fields, bring it all across, and check it landed. Contacts, projects, stages, due dates, file references. Your full history is in the dashboard from day one, and the old system stays running in parallel as a safety net.

"What do we actually own?"

Everything. The agents run on your accounts under your login. The prompts, the workflows, the dashboard, and the data all live in your environment, not ours. If you cancel the retainer, the system keeps running. You lose the upkeep and the new builds. The system itself stays yours.

"Are there other monthly fees?"

The system runs on AI usage billed directly to your own accounts, roughly $100 to $200 a month at typical volume. You own those accounts and see every invoice, with zero markup from us. Often the system replaces tools you're already paying for, so part of that cost cancels out.

"Will AI email our clients without us seeing it?"

Never. Agents draft, they don't send. Everything lands in your dashboard as a draft for review. You approve, edit, or kill it. We can start fully manual, where you approve every single message, and only loosen that once the output has earned your trust.

"Is it safe to let AI near our books?"

Read-only, always. The finance agent reads your accounting software and tells you what matters in plain English. Best and worst months, profit per project, overdue invoices, margin slipping. It never writes to your books. AI can read your numbers and draft entries a human approves. It should never be the source of truth.

"We're not technical. Can our team actually run this?"

Yes. Your team runs everything from the dashboard, no code ever. Every agent comes documented with a walkthrough video, so your people can operate, pause, or adjust it without us.

"Why is the audit only $500?"

Because you shouldn't have to take our word for it. A week of real work for $500 lets you see how we think and what we deliver before committing to a build. It credits toward the build if you continue, and the doc is yours to keep if you don't.

"Can we pay the build over time?"

Yes. The build can be split across three monthly payments, interest-free. If your accounting department needs milestones or a specific structure, we'll work with them.

"What if it doesn't work?"

Two guarantees, both in writing on day one. If everything isn't running by week four, the build is free. And if the system doesn't save you time or make you money, we keep working until it does.
